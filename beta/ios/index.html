pickture on iPhone

Betas are delivered through TestFlight, Apple’s free app for it.

Open the invite

Takes about 2 minutes · Using Android?

  1. 1

    Get TestFlight

    Download TestFlight for free from the App Store.

    Tap Get

    Go to the App Store →
  2. 2

    Open the invite

    Open the invite on your iPhone and accept it in TestFlight.

    Tap Accept

    Open the invite →
  3. 3

    Install

    Install pickture – updates arrive automatically after that.

    Tap Install

  4. 4

    Get started

    pickture is now on your home screen. Have fun!

On a computer? Scan the code with your phone.

Found something? Tell us in the app under Settings → Help → Report a bug, or email support@getpickture.app.
